Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

📋
Overview

VidSave ("we", "our", "us") is an Android app that lets you save videos, music and images from links you paste or share, and from pages you open in its built-in browser. It has no accounts. This policy explains what leaves your phone, why, and what stays on it. We do not sell your personal data.

📦
Data That Leaves Your Phone
  • Links you download. When you paste or share a link and tap Download, the link is sent to a link-resolving service to find the downloadable file: TikWm (tikwm.com) or our own resolving server. Links are processed only to answer that request and are not stored by us or tied to your identity.
  • App usage (screens viewed, downloads started and finished, ads shown, purchases) through Google Firebase Analytics, to understand how features are used.
  • Crash reports (what went wrong in the code, device model, Android and app version) through Google Firebase Crashlytics, to fix crashes.
  • App settings from our server through Google Firebase Remote Config (for example how often ads may appear), which uses an app installation identifier.
  • Advertising data collected by the ad networks listed below (advertising identifier, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions), to show and measure ads. Premium users get no ads.
  • Feedback you choose to send by e-mail from the app, which includes your device model and Android and app version so we can reproduce the problem.

We do not collect your name, e-mail address (unless you write to us), contacts, photos or precise location.

📱
Data That Stays on Your Phone
  • Your downloads, download history, favorites and collections
  • The private folder: hidden files are kept in the app's internal storage behind your PIN or fingerprint; the PIN is stored only as a salted hash
  • In-app browser data: history, bookmarks, open tabs, cookies and site data, and site icons
  • App preferences (theme, language, save folder, default quality)
  • The clipboard: when you open the app, a copied link may be suggested for download. The check happens on your phone; the clipboard is never stored or sent unless you tap Download
✦  Android may back up your app settings to your Google account. Downloads, history, the private folder and browser data are excluded from backups.
🌐
In-App Browser
  • Websites you open load directly from those sites; their own privacy policies and terms apply to what you do there
  • VidSave does not send your browsing history anywhere
  • Private tabs keep no history, and their cookies and site data are deleted when the last private tab closes
  • When you download from a page, your phone fetches the file directly from that site
  • Google does not allow account sign-in inside in-app browsers, so those pages open in your phone's own browser
  • "Clear browsing data" in Settings and "Clear cookies and site data" in a site's info remove this data at any time
🔐
App Permissions
  • Internet / Network state — to resolve links, download files and detect connectivity
  • Notifications — download progress and finished downloads; an occasional reminder if you haven't used the app for a few days (can be turned off in Settings)
  • Foreground service — to keep downloads you started, and music you play, running in the background
  • Fingerprint / screen lock — optional unlock of the private folder (handled by Android; we never see your biometrics)
  • Modify system settings — only when you choose to set a sound as your ringtone
  • Set wallpaper — only when you choose to set an image as wallpaper
  • Storage — only on Android 9 and older, to save files

🎯
Your Choices
  • Ad consent: where required (e.g. the EEA and UK) you are asked before ads are personalized, and you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad Privacy Options
  • Personalized ads: turn them off in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads), and manage Meta ads at facebook.com/adpreferences
  • Reminders: Settings → Daily reminders
  • Local data: Settings → Clear history and Clear browsing data, or uninstall the app
  • No ads at all: Premium, or the free "1 hour without ads" in Settings
🗂️
Retention and Deletion

We keep no copy of the links you download. Analytics and crash data are kept by Google Firebase under its standard retention periods and are tied to app identifiers, not to your name. Because VidSave has no accounts, we cannot look up data by person; uninstalling the app removes its local data and resets its app identifiers, and you can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings. For any request, write to us below.

⭐
Premium Subscriptions
  • Payments are processed by Google Play — we never see your payment details
  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before the renewal date
  • Cancel anytime in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  • Premium status is checked with Google Play Billing and remembered on your phone
👶
Children's Privacy

VidSave is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us.

🛡️
Data Security

Everything the app sends to our server and to TikWm travels over HTTPS. Our server limits or refuses requests that do not come from the VidSave app. Private-folder files are kept in the app's internal storage, which other apps cannot read, and are protected by Android's device encryption; a phone that has been rooted can bypass these protections.
