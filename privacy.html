Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

📋
Overview

VidSave ("we", "our", "us") is a video downloader application for Android that lets you download video and audio content from TikTok, Instagram, and many other supported platforms (YouTube is not supported). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy — VidSave does not collect, sell, or share your personal data, beyond the minimum needed to fetch the content you request, as described below.

📦
Data We Collect

VidSave does not require an account and does not collect or store personal profile data on our servers. Everything except the link-resolving step below stays on your device.

  • The video/audio link you paste or share into the app is sent to our resolving servers (see "Third-Party Services" below) solely to fetch the downloadable file — it is not stored after the request completes and is not linked to your identity
  • App usage data (screens viewed, downloads started/completed, ads shown, purchases made) is collected via Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand feature usage and fix problems — see "Third-Party Services" below
  • Download history is stored locally on your device only
  • App preferences (dark mode, language, save folder) are stored locally
  • No account or registration required
  • We do not collect your name, email, or location
✦  Your download history never leaves your device — only the link you submit is sent out, and only to resolve your download.
🔐
App Permissions
  • Internet — to fetch and download videos
  • Storage — to save downloaded files to your device
  • Notifications — to show download progress
  • Biometric — optional app lock (only if enabled by you)
  • Network State — to detect internet connectivity

Children's Privacy

VidSave is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us immediately.

🛡️
Data Security

VidSave does not transmit your personal profile data (name, email, location, contacts, etc.) to external servers. The only data sent out is the link you submit for download, transmitted over secure HTTPS to our resolving servers described above, solely to fetch your requested content. Downloaded files are stored in the folder you select on your device.
